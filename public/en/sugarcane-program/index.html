Immunity Sugarcane Program

From planting to harvest: the right dose at the right time for every stage.

Short answer

The Immunity Sugarcane Program is a stage-wise program with three products (Immunity Granules, Sugarcane Special and Gold), from planting to the 2–4 leaf stage and repeat sprays.

The whole program in four steps

1

Plantation

Immunity Granules 8–12 KG / vigha (20–30 KG / acre) in the root zone.

2

After the first irrigation

Sugarcane Special drenching 0.5–0.7 L / vigha (3–4 days after herbicide).

3

2–4 Leaves

Special + Gold foliar spray: 250 ML + 250 ML / vigha, 4–5 pumps.

4

Repeat

2–3 sprays every 15–25 days; 1 bag of Granules with every main fertilizer dose.

What stage is your sugarcane at?

🌱 Planting not done yet

  • Granules 8–12 KG / vigha at planting
  • Then early drenching 0.5–0.7 L / vigha
  • Special + Gold spray at 2–4 leaves
  • The next steps of the program every 15–25 days

✅ Planting done

  • Check when the first irrigation is due
  • If herbicide has been applied, drench 3–4 days later
  • Watch for when the crop reaches 2–4 leaves

Frequently asked questions

How many Granules to apply at planting?

Under the current program, apply 8–12 KG of Immunity Granules per vigha (or 20–30 KG per acre) at planting.

When and how much Sugarcane Special drenching?

After the first irrigation / 3–4 days after the herbicide application, 0.5–0.7 litres (500–700 ML) per vigha.

What if herbicide has been applied?

After using herbicide, the advice is to start the drenching program 3–4 days later.

What to spray at the 2–4 leaf stage, and how many pumps?

4–5 spray pumps per vigha. In every 15-litre pump: 45–50 ML Immunity Sugarcane Special + 45–50 ML Immunity Gold (in total 250 ML + 250 ML per vigha).

How often should the spray be repeated?

Usually every 15–25 days, with 2–3 further sprays depending on crop condition.

Should Granules continue after planting?

Yes, as per the program: after that, the advice is to continue with 1 bag of Granules with every main fertilizer dose.
