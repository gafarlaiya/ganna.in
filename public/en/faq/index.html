Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

Short, clear answers about sugarcane and the Immunity Sugarcane Program.

How many Granules to apply at planting?

Under the current program, apply 8–12 KG of Immunity Granules per vigha (or 20–30 KG per acre) at planting.

When and how much Sugarcane Special drenching?

After the first irrigation / 3–4 days after the herbicide application, 0.5–0.7 litres (500–700 ML) per vigha.

What if herbicide has been applied?

After using herbicide, the advice is to start the drenching program 3–4 days later.

What to spray at the 2–4 leaf stage, and how many pumps?

4–5 spray pumps per vigha. In every 15-litre pump: 45–50 ML Immunity Sugarcane Special + 45–50 ML Immunity Gold (in total 250 ML + 250 ML per vigha).

How often should the spray be repeated?

Usually every 15–25 days, with 2–3 further sprays depending on crop condition.

Should Granules continue after planting?

Yes, as per the program: after that, the advice is to continue with 1 bag of Granules with every main fertilizer dose.

Can it be used with chemical fertilizers, fungicides and pesticides?

Yes, it can be used in the program with chemical fertilizers, fungicides and pesticides. If you are unsure about a specific combination, call first.

My sugarcane is already planted. What should I do now?

No need to worry. Call or WhatsApp us with your planting date, variety, current crop stage, irrigation and the fertilizers/sprays applied so far.

How much is needed for 1 acre?

Taking 1 acre = 2.5 vigha: Granules 20–30 KG, Sugarcane Special drenching 1.25–1.75 L, and for the foliar spray 625 ML Special + 625 ML Gold (about 10–12.5 pumps).

Are farmer results guaranteed?

No. Farmer results are their personal experiences. Results can vary with soil, water, variety, weather and management.

Call or WhatsApp: 99787 91167. If you send your planting date, variety, stage and a photo, you will get advice faster.
