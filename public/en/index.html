Common farmer problems

Is adding fertilizer alone enough for sugarcane?

To build a good crop stand you have to look after germination, the root zone, early growth, tillering and plant nutrition together, right from the start.

🌱
Germination gaps

If the early crop stand is not uniform, it can affect the potential of the whole field.

🥀
Tillers / canes dying

If mortality sets in later despite a good start, the plant population can drop.

💰
Chemical costs

If the crop response is not satisfactory even after more inputs, the cost burden rises.

🌍
Soil condition

After years of farming, soil condition and nutrient management need attention.

🌱 Stage-wise Program

Make a strong start right at planting

Instead of doing everything at once, the right dose at the right time for each stage.

01
PLANTATION · IMMUNITY GRANULES

Plantation Dose

8–12 KG / vigha(or 20–30 KG / acre) Use Granules at planting.
  • Apply at the start of planting
  • Distribute well in the root zone
  • Continue the program with later fertilizer doses
02
EARLY CROP CARE · SUGARCANE SPECIAL

Early drenching

0.5–0.7 litres / vigha(500–700 ML / vigha)
  • For drenching after the first irrigation / 3–4 days after the herbicide application
  • Can be used with chemical fertilizers, fungicides and pesticides
  • Deliver it well to the root zone with water
03
2–4 LEAVES · SPECIAL + GOLD

Foliar Spray

250 ML Special + 250 ML Gold / vigha4–5 pumps per vigha (45–50 ML in every 15-litre pump)
  • Spray when the cane is at the 2–4 leaf stage
  • Use the right spray technique for even coverage
04
REPEAT · CONTINUE GRANULES

Repeat Spray + Granules

Every 15–25 days2–3 further sprays depending on crop condition
  • Spray based on growth, colour, tillering and field condition
  • Continue the program with 1 bag of Granules with every main fertilizer dose
⚠️ One important point: drench after the first irrigation / 3–4 days after the herbicide application. Immunity Sugarcane Special, Gold and Granules can generally be used in the program with most chemical fertilizers, fungicides and pesticides. If you are unsure about a tank mix, call us first.

❓ Common farmer questions

Key questions about the program

How many Granules to apply at planting?

Under the current program, apply 8–12 KG per vigha (or 20–30 KG per acre) at planting.

When and how much Sugarcane Special drenching?

For drenching after the first irrigation / 3–4 days after the herbicide application: 0.5–0.7 litres (500–700 ML) per vigha. Can be used with chemical fertilizers, fungicides and pesticides.

What if herbicide has been applied?

After a grass-killer / herbicide, the advice is to start the drenching program 3–4 days later.

What to spray at the 2–4 leaf stage, and how many pumps?

Use 4–5 spray pumps per vigha. In every 15-litre pump: 45–50 ML Immunity Sugarcane Special + 45–50 ML Immunity Gold (in total 250 ML Special + 250 ML Gold per vigha).

How often should the spray be repeated?

Under the current program, usually every 15–25 days, with 2–3 further sprays depending on crop condition.

Can it be used with chemical fertilizer?

Yes, it can be used with chemical fertilizers, fungicides and pesticides. If you are unsure about a particular combination, call and ask.

My sugarcane is already planted. What should I do now?

No need to worry. Share your planting date, variety, current crop stage, irrigation and the fertilizers/sprays applied so far. Call or WhatsApp us to learn the next steps of the program for your crop.

What if I want an exact program for my land?

Call 99787 91167 directly. You can talk to us for guidance that suits your field condition and crop stage.
