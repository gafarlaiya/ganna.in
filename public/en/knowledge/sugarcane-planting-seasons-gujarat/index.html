Sugarcane planting seasons in Gujarat: pre-seasonal, suru and adsali

The three main sugarcane planting seasons in Gujarat, their usual timing and what sets each apart. How Immunity Granules fit in at planting.

Short answer

In Gujarat, sugarcane is usually planted in three seasons: pre-seasonal (October–November), suru (January–February) and adsali (July–August). Decide the exact timing with your region, variety and the sugar factory's advice.

SeasonUsual timingCharacteristicsCrop duration (approx.)
Pre-seasonalOctober–NovemberPlanted before the onset of winterAbout 12 months
SuruJanuary–FebruaryPlanted after winter eases; water planning needed in summerAbout 10–12 months
AdsaliJuly–AugustPlanted in the monsoon; long crop durationAbout 15–18 months

*Timings and durations are approximate. Decide with advice from your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra or sugar factory.

The same sequence in any season

  1. Immunity Granules at planting: 8–12 KG / vigha.
  2. Note the dates of the first irrigation and the herbicide application.
  3. 3–4 days after that, Sugarcane Special drenching: 0.5–0.7 L / vigha.
  4. Special + Gold spray at 2–4 leaves, then repeat every 15–25 days.

Call for advice on your season and variety.

Frequently asked questions

When should sugarcane be planted in Gujarat?

Usually pre-seasonal (October–November), suru (January–February) or adsali (July–August); the exact timing depends on the region and variety.

Why note the date of the first irrigation for drenching?

Because the advice is to do the Sugarcane Special drenching after the first irrigation (or 3–4 days after herbicide).
