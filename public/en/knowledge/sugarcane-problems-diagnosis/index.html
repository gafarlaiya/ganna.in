Common sugarcane problems: from symptom to cause and the first step

Common causes of gaps, yellowing leaves, falling tiller counts, slow growth and drying canes, and the first step for each.

Short answer

Identify the cause from the symptom: gaps, yellowing, falling tiller counts or slow growth can each have different causes. For the right decision, get advice with photos, variety, planting date and stage.

SymptomPossible causesFirst step
Gaps in germinationWeak setts, too little or too much moisture, soil-borne damageInspect the gap spots; gap filling if needed
Leaves turning yellowWater stress or waterlogging, nutrient deficiency, pestsCheck moisture and drainage, then check nutrition/pests
Falling tiller countWater stress, crowding, nutrient imbalancePlan irrigation and nutrition by stage
Slow growthHard soil, low nutrition, pests and diseasesCheck the soil and root zone condition
Canes drying / borers visiblePests such as borersProper control with expert advice

What to send for advice?

  • A clear photo of the problem (of both the field and the plant)
  • Variety and planting date
  • Current crop stage and last irrigation
  • Fertilizers / sprays applied so far

Frequently asked questions

Why do sugarcane leaves turn yellow?

There can be many causes, such as water stress or waterlogging, nutrient deficiency or pests; first check moisture and drainage, then get advice with photos.

What information is needed for advice?

Photos, variety, planting date, current stage, irrigation and the fertilizers/sprays applied so far.
