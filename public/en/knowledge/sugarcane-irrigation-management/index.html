Irrigation management in sugarcane: how much attention at which stage

Planning irrigation for the first watering, tillering, grand growth and maturity, waterlogging problems and drip irrigation.

Short answer

Three stages of sugarcane are sensitive to water: germination, tillering and grand growth. Both water stress and waterlogging can cause damage.

Irrigation by stage

  • First irrigation: to keep moisture after planting. Note the date, because the timing of the herbicide and the drenching is linked to it.
  • Tillering: make sure moisture does not run short. Water stress can affect the tillers.
  • Grand growth: water demand is highest at this stage.
  • Maturity: when to cut back irrigation, as advised by the local sugar factory / expert.

If water stands in the field

First arrange drainage. Standing water can weaken the root zone. Then plan care according to the crop stage; if needed, get advice with photos.

Drip irrigation

Drip irrigation saves water and keeps moisture even. The setup and costs need to be planned in advance.

Irrigation and the Immunity Program

Sugarcane Special drenching is done after the first irrigation (or 3–4 days after herbicide) at 0.5–0.7 L / vigha. Details: Dose schedule.

Frequently asked questions

At which stage does sugarcane need the most water?

Water demand is usually highest during the grand growth stage.

What should I do if water stands in the field?

First arrange drainage, then care for the crop according to its stage; if needed, call 99787 91167 for advice with photos.
