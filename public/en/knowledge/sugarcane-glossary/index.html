Sugarcane glossary: germination, tillering, drenching, foliar spray and other terms

Simple English meanings of the terms used in sugarcane farming: germination, tillering, drenching, foliar spray, Granules, ratoon, suru, adsali.

Short answer

Below are simple meanings of the English and local terms used in sugarcane farming.

Germination
The process in which the buds of planted setts sprout and become new plants.
Tillering (tillers)
New shoots (tillers) emerging from the base of one plant. This is the foundation of the plant population.
Mortality
Tillers or canes dying.
Crop Stand
The overall number of plants in the field and how even they are.
Drenching
A way of delivering a liquid input/nutrition to the root zone along with water.
Foliar Spray
A spray applied to the leaves with a pump.
Granules
A product in granular form, applied to the soil like a fertilizer.
Root Zone
The area of soil where the plant's roots spread.
Ratoon
The second crop that regrows from the stubble after harvest.
Pre-seasonal, suru, adsali
The three sugarcane planting seasons (roughly October–November, January–February, July–August).
Vigha / acre
Land measures. In our calculations we take 1 acre = 2.5 vigha (Gujarat vigha).
