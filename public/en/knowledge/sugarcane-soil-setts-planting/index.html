Sugarcane planting: land preparation, choosing setts and the right method

Land preparation for good germination, choosing healthy setts, even planting and how to apply Granules at planting.

Short answer

Three things matter most for good germination: well-prepared land, healthy setts and even planting with the right moisture.

1. Land preparation

  • Plough deep and make the soil loose and level.
  • Arrange drainage so water does not stand.
  • Mixing in well-rotted farmyard manure / organic manure improves the soil condition; quantity as per local advice.

2. Choosing setts

  • Take fresh, disease-free setts from a reliable source.
  • Usually pieces with 2–3 buds are used.
  • Treat the setts if needed, as per local agricultural advice.

3. Planting

  • Place all setts at an even depth and cover them with soil properly.
  • Keep the moisture right at planting: neither too little nor too much.
  • Keep row and plant spacing according to the variety and method.

4. Granules at planting

Immunity Granules: 8–12 KG / vigha(20–30 KG / acre) Distribute well in the root zone at planting.

Steps after planting: Dose schedule.

Frequently asked questions

How many buds should sugarcane setts have?

Usually pieces with 2–3 buds are used.

Where should Granules be applied at planting?

In the root zone, at the start of planting, 8–12 KG / vigha (20–30 KG / acre), distributed evenly.
