Immunity Sugarcane Program: a complete stage-wise guide from planting to the 2–4 leaf stage

13 October 2026 · Gujarat Bio Organics Pvt. Ltd.

The Immunity Sugarcane Program is built on three products: Immunity Granules, Immunity Sugarcane Special and Immunity Gold. Each is used according to the crop stage.

StageProductDose (per vigha)
1. PlantationImmunity Granules8–12 KG (20–30 KG / acre)
2. After the first irrigation / 3–4 days after herbicideSugarcane Special (Drenching)0.5–0.7 Litres (500–700 ML)
3. 2–4 LeavesSugarcane Special + Immunity Gold (Foliar)250 ML + 250 ML, 4–5 pumps (45–50 ML / 15 L pump)
4. RepeatFoliar spray + Granules2–3 sprays every 15–25 days; 1 bag of Granules with every main fertilizer dose

Precautions

  • If herbicide has been used, drench 3–4 days after it.
  • Keep the spray coverage even.
  • Can be used in the program with chemical fertilizers, fungicides and pesticides; call first about any specific tank mix.
  • Use the live dose calculator to find the quantities for your field.

What if planting is already done?

No need to worry. Call or WhatsApp us with your planting date, variety, current stage, irrigation and the fertilizers/sprays applied so far; we will explain the next steps of the program accordingly.

*This information is general guidance and current program guidance. Results can vary with soil, variety, water, weather and management; no result is guaranteed. Call for advice that suits your field conditions.
